Colour proof

The mockup's original chart hues could not ship: its blue and purple are separated by ΔE 0.1 for deutan viewers — the same colour to roughly 8% of men — and ΔE 10.2 even with full colour vision, under the floor of 15. These four are the same blue-led family, re-stepped until every check passes.

Profit green #0f9d58 and loss red #d92d3f are kept from the original, but they sit at ΔE 4.0 for deutan viewers — the classic red/green trap. They are only legal because every mark carrying them also carries a signed number: each waterfall bar is labelled, each P&L figure shows its minus sign. That labelling is load-bearing, not decoration — if a future card drops it, the colour stops meaning anything.

Type is the system UI stack rather than a webfont, deliberately: React Native renders system faces on device, so a mockup in anything else would misrepresent what ships. Every figure is set in tabular numerals so columns align.

What changed, and what didn't

New: the P&L card, the revenue→profit waterfall, and payment methods — the last reusing PaymentMixChart, which already exists in Accounting. The greeting sentence is generated from figures the screen already holds.

Restyled, same data: revenue trend, goal meter, needs attention, low stock. The uncosted-cost caveat becomes the amber banner and keeps its deep link to the inventory filter.

Deliberately absent: cash on hand, budget vs actual, recurring bills, sales tax, and the expense donut. They stay in Accounting — on a new shop's dashboard they would be five empty states above the fold.
