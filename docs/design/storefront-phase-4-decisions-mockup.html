Regression from Decision 4 · fixed

Three prose blocks, one left edge

At 1440 the FAQ's question cards sat at x=326 while the proof chips and the story card sat at x=310. At 390 both paths coincidentally landed on 32, so only a wide screen showed it.

Cause. Two of the three blocks sit inside the panel, whose padding lands outside the 820 bound. The FAQ sat outside the panel and recreated the inset with its own gutter, which lands inside it — a leftover from before Task 21, when the story was a full-bleed band and every band needed its own inset. The story was folded into a card inside the panel; this band was never migrated with it.

Fix. The FAQ is now a plain fourth child of the panel and the three leftover styles are deleted, so one container owns the inset — this codebase's own recorded rule: the gutter belongs to the container that knows its own margins. Measured after, at both widths: one left edge and one right edge (32/358 at 390, 310/1130 at 1440), with the gallery still full-bleed at 1376.

No test here lays out, so nothing could have caught the pixels — a browser did. What is pinned instead is the cause: none of the three bounded blocks may carry horizontal padding of its own. Restoring the gutter fails it.
