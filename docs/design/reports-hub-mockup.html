The Reports hub, and the seven that need no ledger

Reports is still the P&L / tax / category tab it has always been. This turns it into a hub like Accounting's, and builds the reports that read tables kaiibi already has — no ledger, no cost layers, no migration. The statements stay where they are until the posting phase gives them something real to read.

The seven are the whole of this project. Everything else on the hub renders as a card that says what it is waiting for, rather than a link to an empty screen. A card that opens nothing is worse than a card that explains itself.
