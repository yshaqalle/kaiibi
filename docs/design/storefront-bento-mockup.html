Storefront · design

The app has a design system. The storefront never got it.

Dashboard and Accounting are Bento: a page tone, borderless 26px cards floating on it, one inverted anchor card, an eyebrow-and-value type ramp. The storefront predates that and is still a phone layout stretched across a laptop. This redraws it in Bento — the same surfaces and the same type ramp the shopkeeper already uses, recoloured into their own palette.

What carries over, and how

Bento is a surface system, not a colour scheme — which is exactly why it survives being recoloured six ways. Every row below is an existing token or an existing component role.

In the appOn the storefrontWhere it comes from
Grey page, white cards
bentoPage / bentoSurface		 The palette's soft is the page; ground is the card. A 3% step — the same distance #f4f4f5 sits from #ffffff. Both already in storefront-catalog.ts. No new stored value.
The one dark card
Takings, on Dashboard		 The shop itself. Wordmark, place, headline, and the WhatsApp button, on ink with ground type. The page finally has a centre of gravity instead of a field of cream. Contrast is already proven: every palette's ink clears 7:1 on its ground, and inverting a pair does not change the ratio.
Eyebrow / value ramp
10.5px·800·tracked, then 34px·800·−1.1		 Section heads and the price. A price is the number the customer came for — it should be set like the app sets a figure. Replaces four ad-hoc sizes in scale.ts.
Borderless, radius 26 Every card and product tile. Product photos inset at 18. BENTO_RADIUS. Drops the 1px tile border the grid has today.
The dot row
“7/7 products sold have a cost set”		 A dot per product, hollow when it is out of stock. Honest and legible at three items and at thirty. Same card shape, new content. Shape carries the state, colour is the second signal — the rule storefront-catalog.ts already sets.
Black pill button
“Show my tasks · 2”		 Cart and Checkout. WhatsApp keeps its fixed green — a recognised affordance, never a brand colour. WHATSAPP_BUTTON_GREEN, unchanged.
What Bento does not bring. No BentoGrid import — that component pins Colors.light, and this page renders in one of six palettes for a stranger who has no account. The storefront takes the system: the surfaces, the radius, the ramp, the anchor card. It keeps its own tokens. Same reason StatTile and Badge cannot be dropped on it as-is — the skill already flags that they hardcode a palette.

Card by card

The shopDark, full width. Wordmark, place, headline, about. On a phone the WhatsApp button moves up to the button row, where it already lives — not repeated in the card.
CollectingHalf width. The three lines a customer asks before they order.
In the shopHalf width. Count, then a dot per product. Reads at a glance whether the shop is stocked.
ProductsTwo columns, unchanged from today's breakpoints. Only the card styling changes.
Checkout barThe ink pill, still absolute, still gone at an empty cart.

A hero photograph, when the shop has uploaded one, becomes the dark card's background with the scrim already in theme-window.tsx — the type is already white on it, so that branch needs no new colour and no new rule.

What this costs in code

No migration, no new column, no new dependency, no change to the palette set or the contrast tests. One derived token, and it is derived the way mutedInk already is.

FileChange
storefront-catalog.ts Add onDarkMuted — ground blended 34% toward ink, the mirror of mutedInk. Contrast-tested alongside it, against ink rather than ground.
scale.ts Replace the ad-hoc sizes with the Bento ramp: eyebrow 10.5·800·+1, value 34·800·−1.1, price 23·800·−0.8, body 13. Add SHOP_MAX_WIDTH = 1080 and RADIUS = { card: 26, inset: 18, pill: 999 }.
theme-shared.tsx New ShopPage (page tone, 1,080 column) and ShopCard (borderless, radius 26). New ShopAnchor, CollectingCard and StockCard. Grid rule fixed so a short final row pads rather than flexes.
theme-window.tsx, theme-market.tsx, theme-counter.tsx Each renders the three cards, then its own body. Window's hero photo becomes the anchor card's background, scrim and ON_SCRIM_INK unchanged.
product-tile.tsx Card at 26, photo inset at 18, price on the value ramp, reserved name slot, Add/Ask as pills. Border removed.
category-band.tsx Tiles become the pill row above. The four accent shades and the two-row cap go — a pill row scrolls and needs neither.
One judgement call worth naming. The category band today is four photo-sized tiles in shades of the accent; here it is a pill row. Pills are what the app uses for the same job (the range and tab rows on Dashboard), they scroll rather than capping at four, and they stop the band from outweighing the goods it is a route into. If you want the picture tiles kept, that is the one piece to say so about — everything else in this mockup is the app's own vocabulary applied unchanged.
