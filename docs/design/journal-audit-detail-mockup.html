Accounting · Ledger · design proposal

Opening a journal entry, and an audit log row

Both lists become clickable. Journals opens the whole entry: what caused it, every line with its account, what it means in plain words, and who posted it. The audit log does the same for each change and stops listing each entry line as its own row.

All figures are from the yusef@gmail.com shop (JE-2026-0063, JE-2026-0064), except the reversal pair in section 3, which uses the JE-2026-0078 amounts you pasted. The other list rows' amounts and customer names are placeholders.

On a wide screen the list stays put and the detail docks on the right, so you can walk down the entries with the pane open. The row's UUID description is replaced by who and what: "Sale · QA Amina Hersi" rather than "Sale 6669e945-…".

Decisions for you

Fold entry lines into their entry in the audit log?

Recommended: yes. 283 of 351 rows are lines, and they're never meaningful on their own. They stay reachable under "Show raw record".

Replace UUIDs in entry descriptions with names?

Recommended: yes, in the app only. "Sale · QA Amina Hersi" is looked up when shown; the stored description is left alone, since posted entries can't be edited.

"In plain words" — keep it?

Generated from the account types (receivable, revenue, tax, stock, cost). It's the part aimed at someone who doesn't read debits and credits. Easy to leave out if it feels like too much.

Where a reversal made today shows up

Recommended: keep its accounting date, but add a "Written in this range" toggle to Journals so today's deletions of older sales are one tap away.

Clicking an account (your earlier "click the ledgers")

Not in this mockup. The same pane, opened from Chart of Accounts or Trial Balance, would list every line that makes up that account's balance, each opening its entry. Say if you want it drawn next.
