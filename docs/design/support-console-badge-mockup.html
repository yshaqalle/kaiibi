The store gets a mark on its ☰ when we write to them. We get nothing when they write to us. This is what that costs, what fixing it takes, and the reason I am not sure it is worth building.

2 · Why it cannot just be switched on

A badge has to subtract what you have already seen. Nothing currently records that.

-- what the database records today operator SENDS a reply → platform_read_at = now() ✓ written operator OPENS a thread → nothing happens -- so "unread" would mean "I have never replied to this", -- and a thread you read and decided needed no answer -- would stay lit up forever
⚠Without a mark-on-open, the badge is not a count of what needs attention — it is a count of what you have not typed into. Those are different, and the difference is exactly the threads you looked at and judged fine.

Build it if…

You keep the console open on Overview or Stores while doing other work, and want support to interrupt you.

Volume grows past a handful a week, where "I'll check Support when I think of it" starts dropping things.

More than one operator — then "have I looked at this" stops being answerable from memory.

Skip it if…

You open the console to do support, in which case the sort has already done the job before a badge could have told you anything.

You would rather not maintain a second source of truth about what is unanswered — a badge that disagrees with the list is worse than no badge.

Anything genuinely urgent reaches you on WhatsApp anyway, which is the same reason the store's own unread count was built best-effort.

My read

Skip it for now. With one operator and today's volume, the sort covers it, and the badge introduces a "read" concept the system does not otherwise have — which is a small amount of state that can drift from the thing it describes. It becomes clearly worth building at the point where a second operator exists, or where you notice yourself missing things. Neither is true yet.
