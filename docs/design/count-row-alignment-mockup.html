Three heights, bottom‑aligned The label‑plus‑field stack is ~50px, the variance ~16px, the chip ~28px. alignItems: 'flex-end' settles them on a shared bottom edge, nowhere near the middle of the box.
The cap repeats on every row Nine‑pixel COUNTED over each field — the noise and the cause, since it is what makes the first child taller than everything beside it.
The variance floats between two boxes A bare number sandwiched between a filled field and a filled pill. It reads as a stray annotation rather than the third column it is.
Products run together One grey block, hairline rules, no gaps. Scanning a count of twelve, there is nothing to tell you where one product ends and the next begins.

The card is the separator A white row on the grey panel, 8px of air between each. Down a count of twelve, one product is one card — no hunting for where the next begins.
Three boxes, one height Field, variance and reason are all 38px and vertically centred. The eye reads three columns instead of a box, a number and a pill at three different altitudes.
The tint carries the direction Short is red on a red wash, over is green on green, unchanged is plain grey — so a shortfall is findable at a glance, and colour is never the only signal because the sign is right there.
Fixed widths, stable edges 62 / 58 / 108. −12 occupies the same column as −1, and “Theft or loss” the same as “Reason” — so nothing shifts as the count is filled in.

What changes, exactly

Six style edits and one small piece of markup. No new state and no behaviour change — the reason chips still unfold inline beneath the row, which is the part iOS cannot do any other way.

StyleFromToWhy
qtyPair alignItems: 'flex‑end' alignItems: 'center' The variance and the chip centre on the field instead of hanging off its bottom edge.
cap once per row, above each field once per basket, as a header Removes the height mismatch that caused the misalignment, and stops repeating a label twelve times down a long count.
varianceText bare Text, minWidth 30 a 58×38 box, tinted by direction Gives the middle column the same weight as its neighbours, and makes a shortfall findable by colour as well as by sign.
reasonChip hugs its text, ~28px tall width 108, height 38 Matches the other two boxes, and holds the row’s right edge still when a reason is picked.
lineWrap hairline rule between rows a card, with 8px gaps One product reads as one thing.
qtyInput grey fill on a grey panel white fill on a grey card The field has to stay the one thing that looks typeable now that the card behind it is grey.

One thing to decide

The header needs three words. COUNTED is already the shipped one. For the other two I have drawn OFF BY and WHY — short enough not to wrap at phone width, and plain rather than accounting language: a shop reads “off by −2”, not “variance”.

If you would rather the middle column stayed unlabelled, dropping OFF BY costs nothing — the tint and the sign already say what it is. WHY is the one worth keeping, because an untouched row shows a chip reading “Reason” and nothing else explains what that button is for.
