Retiring a plan

Turning off a tier so no new store can pick it, giving the stores already on it a month and a warning, and moving them to a named successor when the month runs out. Every frame below is pinned to Colors.light, the palette the app actually renders.

The gap today

plans.is_public already exists and listPlans() already filters on it — but nothing in the portal can set it. Retiring Free meant editing hardcoded copy and shipping a build (commit 0be1bae), which is exactly what putting plans in a table was meant to avoid.

Already correct — don't touch it

The trial credit works. RecordPayment starts the paid period at the latest of today, current cover, and trial end, and the "give up their free days" toggle is off by default. Two small hardening fixes are listed at the end; the behaviour itself stays.

Trial is struck through: it is is_public: false and assigned by trigger, so it can never be a successor. A plan that is itself retiring is struck through for the same reason — the resolver follows one hop only, so chains are refused at the door rather than looped over at read time.

The one open decision

Where do lapsed stores land once Free is gone?

shop_effective_status is dates-only, so a Free store already derives to expired and reaches Free by falling through platform_settings.post_trial_plan_key. Retire Free with Standard as successor and every lapsed store on the platform quietly gains Standard's entitlements. The frames above assume a new Starter floor — a smaller free tier — set in the same action. If the floor should be something else, sections 2 and 4 change.
