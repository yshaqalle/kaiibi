Colour for the two hubs

Reports and The books render the same card sheet — a grey tile, a grey action pill, and eleven of them stacked in bands. Nothing on either page tells you where you are without reading. This proposes four marks, one per band, and four ways of spending them.

The palette

Four marks, not eleven. Every hue is one the theme has already solved and shipped — three of the four categorical series plus the ink — so this adds two wash/ink pairs to theme.ts and reuses the rest.

MarkCarriesOn which hubWhy that hueTokens
Blue The hub's own core work Sales · Ledger and journals Already the app's data ink. Each hub's biggest band is the one you came for. bentoSeries1 · bentoAccentWash/Ink (exist)
Teal Things the shop holds Inventory · Assets Stock and fixed assets are the same idea at two speeds, so they take one mark. bentoSeries2 · new wash/ink
Amber Has a clock on it Customers and suppliers · Oversight Aging, closing a period, the audit trail. Amber is already the app's third status: needs noticing, not broken. bentoSeries3 · bentoRefundWash/Ink (exist)
Graphite The formal statements Financial statements (both hubs) The one band that appears on both, so it must read the same on both. Black rather than a fifth hue — see below. bentoInk · bentoSoft (exist)

Why four and not five. The first cut gave Financial statements an indigo and Customers a pink. Both died on the colour-vision check: indigo against the blue is ΔE 1.5 for a deutan viewer, and the teal and pink washes are ΔE 0.9 — two bands that are simply the same colour for roughly one man in twelve. The set above is the four the theme already validated, and every pair clears ΔE 52+ under both deutan and protan simulation.

The hue is never the only cue. A group heading sits directly above every band and each card carries its own name, so the colour is wayfinding, not information — it is not doing the job bentoProfit/bentoLoss do, and does not need their sign-or-glyph rule.

The recommendation

C, with one caveat.

C separates the two questions a card answers. The tile says what kind of thing this is and the pill says this is the press — so the pressable thing is one colour on every card of both hubs, and scanning for a button never means scanning for four colours. It also gets the only genuinely useful distinction on these pages for free: a solid blue pill for the four cards that create something (+ New entry, + Post history, + Add asset, + Close a month) against a washed pill for the ones that only open a screen. The books already draws that split — it ships a creates flag and a solid black pill — but black is a third colour beside the grey one and says nothing about the press. This keeps the distinction and makes it two weights of the one blue, so the pill's hue means “press me” and its weight means “this one writes”. Reports has no create cards at all, so every pill there is washed.

The caveat. Solid tiles are the loudest option here, and the Reports hub shows twelve at once. If that reads as a toybox on a real screen, A is the same scheme at a whisper — the band mapping and the tokens are unchanged, only the tile fill drops from solid to wash — and it keeps C's blue action pill if you want the two halves separately.

D is here to be rejected. Colouring the button by band is the one arrangement that actively misleads: it makes the pressable element change colour for reasons that have nothing to do with what pressing it does, and it puts amber on a button that opens a list.

What ships

Five tokens, one shared helper, both hubs. Shipped on accounting-hub-colour — the frames above are the design, and the built screens match them.
