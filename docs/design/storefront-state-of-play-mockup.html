Kaiibi · storefront · state of play

What the storefront is now, what its address looks like, and where a flyer would sit

Four plans shipped and a fifth is in review. This is the whole thing on one page — the address a shop hands to a customer, the two journeys that already work end to end, and the two gaps left.

The address

A subdomain, not a path. Getting this backwards is a mistake this codebase has already made once.

https://xamdi-electronics.kaiibi.com
The shop chooses the blue part, once. 3–63 characters, lowercase, digits and hyphens. One label — no dots inside it.
kaiibi.com/xamdi-electronics  — never this. The resolver only ever accepts a subdomain, so a path printed on a shop card is an address that does not work.

Every host serves the same bundle, so the hostname is the only thing telling xamdi-electronics.kaiibi.com from the admin app. The resolver fails closed: anything it does not positively recognise as one label plus the domain loads the normal app instead. That is why localhost and preview URLs never accidentally serve a shop's page — and why a.b.kaiibi.com resolves to nothing rather than guessing which half is the shop.

Names a shop cannot take

About twenty are reserved, enforced in one place and read by both the app and the database so the two cannot drift: www · app · api · admin · platform · dashboard · account · accounts · billing · support · help · status · blog · docs · mail · smtp · ftp · cdn · static · assets

The two journeys that already work

A customer

1Opens the link

From a WhatsApp message or a printed card.

2Browses

Only items the shop switched on for online. Out-of-stock says so.

3Basket, or asks

Add to basket to buy, or Ask to open WhatsApp about that one item.

4Checks out

Name, phone, collect or deliver, a landmark. No card — payment happens in person.

5Gets a number

"#7. We'll call you when it's ready."

The shop

1New

The order lands in the inbox with what was asked for and the total.

2Accept

Or cancel with a reason the customer can be told.

3Ready

Picked and waiting. The shop calls.

4Complete

Asks how they paid — cash, ZAAD, e-Dahab.

5It becomes a sale

Stock drops, the journal balances, the delivery fee posts to 4300.

Step 5 is the one worth pausing on. An order is not money — until it completes, the inbox says so in as many words: "$23.00 across 1 order still open is what customers have asked for, not money the shop has taken." Completion is what puts it in the books, and it is the same posting path a counter sale uses, so the two cannot disagree.

1 · Who — the shop's name and town

A customer arriving from a forwarded link needs to know they are in the right place before anything else happens.

2 · Where — blurb and opening hours

Already there. In Hargeisa the hours and the landmark are half the reason the page exists.

3 · What's on — the flyers

This is the new band. It sits directly above the goods it points at, so tapping it and arriving at the solar items is one continuous movement. Putting it above the shop's name would make the loudest thing on the page arrive before the customer knows whose page it is.

The fourth surface for one offer: the till applies it, the poster prints it, a campaign sends it, this shows it — all reading the same promotion row.

4 · Goods

Unchanged. Only items the shop switched on for online, priced from the same products the till uses.

This places the band lower than the first sketch of it did, which put flyers immediately under the header. Tying the poster to the goods beats tying it to the masthead.

What a flyer is, and is not

QuestionAnswer
How many?Up to five. None renders nothing at all — no empty frame, no "add a flyer" placeholder facing a customer. One renders static, with no dots and no arrows, because a carousel of one with controls nobody can use is a lie about what is there.
Can it be tapped?Three choices: nothing, show a category, or ask on WhatsApp about that offer. A flyer that cannot be acted on is decoration.
Does it move?Not unless the shop turns it on, and never for a customer whose device asks for reduced motion — their preference beats the shop's setting. It stops for good the moment somebody touches or focuses it.
Where do the images live?The same upload path logos and product photos already use. No fourth one, and no new bucket.
Can it be prepared in advance?Yes — the editor already has a draft column, so next week's poster can exist without being live.
Does it know about a real offer?It must, when it claims one. A flyer carries the same optional link to a promotion that a printed poster and a WhatsApp campaign already do. With one attached, the offer's words — "20% off solar, through Saturday" — are derived from that row, not typed, so the page cannot advertise a discount the till will not give. Only the headline is free text, because "Ciid wanaagsan" is not derivable from a discount row. With none attached it is a plain announcement: new stock, a change of hours.

The flyer reads the promotion; it never applies one. Nothing about the discount engine changes.

What is left

GapWhat it costsState
Address follows the name No schema. Editor logic plus a suffix field. The address is derived while unclaimed and frozen once claimed — a rename must never move a link already printed on a card. Drawn, not planned
Flyers One table, one boolean, and the existing public read extended rather than a fourth one added — an unpublished shop and an unknown name have to stay indistinguishable, which is only true if flyers travel on the same call. Drawn, not planned
Paying online Deliberately not built. The column that would carry it exists and permits one value, so adding a provider later does not need a migration to the orders table. Not planned
Refunding a delivery fee "Does returning one item out of a five-item delivery refund the trip?" is a product question, not a schema one. Deferred, on purpose
Scheduling a flyer Worth wanting, much bigger. Draft plus a tap runs a week's promotion today. Out of scope
Redirecting an old address Needs a history table and a resolver that fails open — the opposite of how the current one is built, and the reason it is safe. Out of scope
