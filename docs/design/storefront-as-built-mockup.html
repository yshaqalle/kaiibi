What still needs improving

Ranked by what would actually change the scoreboard.

1 · A shop working the till never learns an order arrived

The waiting-order count sits on the Orders row inside the ☰ menu, and on the Dashboard's task count. The ☰ button itself carries no badge. A shopkeeper spends the day on POS — so nothing on screen tells them a customer is waiting until they wander back to the Dashboard or open a menu for another reason.

Fix: put the count on the ☰ button, which is visible on every screen. Small, and it is the difference between an order inbox and an order notification.

2 · Standard has no storefront, and seven trials lapse in ten weeks

Trial and Pro include it; Free and Standard do not. Seven of eleven shops are on Trial — one already expired, the rest ending 2 Nov – 8 Dec. When one lapses or converts to Standard it loses the page it published, and the address it printed stops being reachable from the app.

Fix: one line of plans.modules — but it is a pricing decision, not an engineering one. It is the only open item with a deadline.

3 · Listing forty products means forty forms

"Sell online" is a per-product toggle. There is no bulk action, deliberately: a shop publishing its whole catalogue by accident is a decision, not a convenience. But four products listed across eleven shops suggests the current cost is too high.

Fix: multi-select on the filtered "Not online" list, with an explicit confirm naming the count. Half a day.

4 · Native is unverified

The nav and share changes are Jest-verified on native and browser-verified on web. Nobody has seen them on a real phone, because testing native needs port 8081 and another session held it.

Ten minutes on a free machine. Not a risk to correctness, but it is not "tested".

Left outWhy
Paying before collection Needs no provider — shops already store their ZAAD/e-Dahab merchant numbers and receipts print them. The cost is that it moves risk to a customer with no recourse. A decision, not a build.
Scheduling a flyerDraft plus a tap runs a week's promotion today.
Flyers on the Counter layoutThat layout exists to make a 200-line price list readable. A poster fights it.
Refunding a delivery fee"Does returning one item out of five refund the trip?" is a product question.
Redirecting an old addressNeeds a resolver that fails open — the opposite of what makes the current one safe.

The honest summary: the storefront is finished and unused, and the four fixes above are worth roughly a day between them. None of them is likely to be why nobody has published. That answer is with the shopkeepers, not in the code.
