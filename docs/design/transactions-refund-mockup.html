Saying a sale came back

Two things are wrong on this tab. One is arithmetic and is already fixed: a refunded sale reported minus its own tax as revenue and profit. The other is that nothing on the list tells you a sale was refunded until you open it.

1. The arithmetic, before and after

A $12.60 line, taxed to $12.92, refunded in full. The refund handed back all $12.92 — tax included — but revenue had already excluded the tax, so subtracting the refund whole took the tax out twice and left the sale at exactly minus its own tax.

The Refunded block is the point

Showing $0.00 without saying why just moves the confusion. The block names the money that went back and splits the tax out of it, so the reader can see the two figures that used to collide.

Payment still says $12.92

That is what was rung up and what the till saw. It is not corrected downward, because the refund is a separate event with its own date — the same reason refunds book to the day they happened everywhere else.

Today a refund shows as · 1↩ appended to the Payment column, sharing that cell with the edit glyph · 1✎. It is in the wrong column, at 13.5px, and it is the only thing distinguishing a sale that was fully handed back from one that stuck.

Partial reads differently from full

"1 of 4 back" is the fact a shopkeeper actually needs; "1↩" made a one-unit return and a whole-basket return look identical. The two states get two washes, not one.

Costs the Items column room

Items is 26% of the row. A long product name plus a badge will truncate sooner. The badge is flex:none so the name yields first, never the badge.

Too loud for how often refunds happen

A tinted row is the strongest signal the table has, and it is spent on a state that is normal. On a busy day the ledger stripes and the reader stops seeing it.

The wash collides with row selection

bentoSoft already marks the selected/expanded row. Two fills competing on the same row means the expanded refunded row has to pick one, and either choice loses a state.

"$69.30 kept" is more useful than any badge — it is the figure the reader would other­wise open the row to compute.

Breaks the column and the sort

Total is sortable. Two numbers in one cell means the header sorts by a figure that is no longer the only one shown, and every refunded row grows to two lines — the row height stops being uniform down a ledger that is read by scanning.

4. Recommendation — A, with C's number moved into the open row

The badge marks the state where the eye already is; the kept figure appears in the detail pane, where there is room for it and where it can be labelled. Nothing is struck through and no column changes width.

"Kept" is the row's own reconciliation

Payment $12.92, refunded −$12.92, kept $0.00. Three lines that add up, which is what the −$0.32 never did. On the partial row it reads $92.40 / −$23.10 / $69.30.

Edited becomes a pill too

Not strictly in scope, but · 1✎ is in the same overloaded cell. Leaving it as a glyph while the refund becomes a pill makes the edit state look like the lesser fact, which it is not. Cheap to do at the same time.

5. What it costs

One new token pair. The other two are already in theme.ts.

↩ Aa bentoRefundWash / bentoRefundInk

New. Amber, because a refund is the third status — needs noticing, not broken. Red would say the sale is a loss and would cry wolf beside a genuinely negative profit; green is nonsense. Same family as bentoWarn, washed to the lightness of the existing badge pairs. The exact steps need the contrast solve the others got — bentoUpInk and bentoDownInk are both documented at 4.5:1 on their own wash, and this pair has to clear the same bar before it ships.

↩ Aa bentoAccentWash / bentoAccentInk

Already exists, already documented as "a figure being carried beside a status one, with no status of its own" — which is exactly a partial refund: informational, not a state the sale is in.

✎ Aa bentoSoft / bentoMuted

Already exists. The edit pill stays neutral — an edit is bookkeeping, not money moving.

Components: the pill is a new bento variant on Badge, which the skill already flags as hardcoding the cream palette and needing one. This gives it a first caller rather than adding a one-off View to the row.

Not changed: the Total column, which keeps meaning what was rung up; the Refunds issued line on Reports, which keeps meaning what was handed across the counter; and the sort, which still has one number per row to sort by.
