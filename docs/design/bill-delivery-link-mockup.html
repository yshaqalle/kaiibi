A bill names the delivery it pays for

receive_stock posts Dr 1200 Inventory / Cr 2000 Accounts Payable — goods arrived, the supplier is owed. record_invoice_payment posts Dr 2000 / Cr wallet — the supplier is paid. Those two only net out if the bill being paid is for the delivery that raised the payable, and until now the app guessed at that by category. This gives the bill a column that says which delivery, and shuts the door on the case where there isn't one.

1 · Why the category was a guess

Today post_expense_to_ledger reads the category off the bill's mirrored expenses row. An inventory_purchase bill posts nothing, on the assumption that a delivery already raised its payable. That assumption is a guess about something the database was never told, and it is wrong in both directions.

What really happenedCategory tappedWhat posts todayResult
Delivery recorded in Restock; bill for it entered after inventory_purchase Delivery: Dr 1200 / Cr 2000. Bill: nothing. Correct. This is the app's own unpaid-delivery flow.
Goods bill entered with no delivery behind it inventory_purchase Nothing at all. Under-stated. Nothing ever credits 2000, so paying it drives Accounts Payable into debit. A negative liability on a balance sheet.
Delivery recorded; bill for it entered under supplies supplies Delivery: Dr 1200 / Cr 2000. Bill: Dr 6400 / Cr 2000. Over-stated. The payable is doubled, the cost is on the P&L and the goods are on the balance sheet, and paying once clears half.

Both rows are the same root cause and every entry involved balances, so the trial balance zeroes throughout — the same reason none of the earlier defects on this branch were caught by a totals check. And the two cases cannot be told apart by category, because in one of them the category is exactly what is wrong. That is the argument for a link: a fact, recorded, rather than an inference.

The same shape has already bitten this branch once.

PR #74 removed a wholesale invoice_id exclusion whose stated premise — “the bill recognised the cost” — turned out to be false: nothing on the branch posted when an invoice was inserted, and the row being skipped was the recognition. A guess about what another door did, written as a comment and trusted for four migrations. This is a second instance of it, and the fix is to stop guessing.

2 · The decision — shut the door, do not post around it

The obvious move once invoices.stock_receipt_id exists is: linked bills post nothing, unlinked ones post Dr 1200 / Cr 2000. That is wrong, and it is worth writing out why, because it is the branch a reader will reach for first.

Rejected
An unlinked goods bill posts Dr 1200 / Cr 2000

The trigger cannot tell the two unlinked cases apart, which is the whole problem it was meant to solve, arriving one layer down.

Case A — the goods DID arrive and were
recorded in Restock; only the link is missing.
  delivery: Dr 1200 4,800 / Cr 2000 4,800
  bill:     Dr 1200 4,800 / Cr 2000 4,800
  → stock and payable both DOUBLED.

Case B — the goods arrived and were never
recorded at all. products.stock knows nothing.
  bill:     Dr 1200 4,800 / Cr 2000 4,800
  → ledger value rises, quantity does not.

Case B is worse than it looks. opening_inventory_gap is stock on hand less what the ledger holds against 1200, so a phantom debit shrinks the opening balance by exactly the phantom — permanently, because the opening marker means a second replay can never correct it. That is the negative-asset failure 20260908001800 was written to remove, re‑entering from the other end.

Built
A goods bill cannot be entered without one

The link is required at insert for inventory_purchase, offered for every other category, and immutable afterwards. What a bill posts is decided once, when it is entered.

linked (any category) → posts nothing.
  the delivery already raised the payable.

unlinked, inventory_purchase → posts nothing.
  unreachable for a bill entered today — the
  door refuses it. Rows predating the door
  reach here, and there is no honest entry
  for them. An admitted gap.

unlinked, anything else → Dr category / Cr 2000.

The link is tested first, so a goods bill mis‑tapped as supplies and linked posts nothing and the over‑stated direction closes too. The category arm survives only as a residual for rows the link cannot describe, and it no longer claims that a delivery recognised anything.

Why an admitted gap beats a precise-looking number.

In case B the shop's stock records are already wrong whatever the ledger does — units are on the shelf that products.stock has never heard of. Posting Dr 1200 does not fix that; it hides it, by making the value side agree with a quantity that is missing. Today's negative payable is at least visible, and PR #76 put a wrong‑toned caveat on it. 20260804000000's header states the house instinct plainly: a precise-looking number that is simply wrong is worse than an admitted gap.

Why the link is immutable.

The reverse‑and‑re‑post triggers on expenses fire on a fixed column list (20260908001000), and no column on expenses changes when the invoice's link does — so the posting would not follow the link even if we let it move. A mutable link would mean the live entry was written under one answer while the replay reads another, which is the one property this whole phase is built on. Set once, at insert, and the two can never disagree. Correcting a mis‑picked delivery is delete‑and‑re‑enter, which already reverses the bill and its payments together (Task 7d).

One new field, between the store picker and the category chips. It reads “Not for a delivery” by default and is available on every category, not just the goods one — because the over‑stated case is precisely a goods bill wearing another category, and a field that only appeared for inventory_purchase would never be shown to the person making that mistake.

4 · When there is no delivery to link

Two shapes, and they need different sentences. A shop with no unbilled deliveries at all is being told to go and record one. A shop whose delivery exists but was received with no costs on it is being told something narrower — that delivery never reached the books either, so linking to it would settle a payable nobody raised.

The caveat keeps wrong and keeps its action.

The figure on screen is still wrong and the remedy is still live: for a bill whose delivery genuinely never happened, recording it credits 2000 by the delivery's value and corrects stock at the same time. One clause is added — “New bills for goods can no longer be entered this way” — so a reader who fixes it once knows it will not silently come back. The hasUnposted branch above it, which points at Post History instead, is untouched: before history is posted the diagnosis is still not exclusive, and “record the delivery” for an old rent bill would have the shop invent goods that never arrived.

The per-bill note is not a Caveat.

It is a badge and a line of meta inside the row, because a Caveat is a qualification on a figure the card is showing and this is a property of one row among many. Seven of them stacked in a list would be seven amber blocks and the reader would stop seeing any of them — the failure mode the tone system exists to avoid.

Nothing offers to link an existing bill.

Because the column is immutable, and a control that appeared to fix something and silently did not would be worse than no control. The row names the two real remedies in words instead. Both work today: recording the delivery credits 2000; deleting the bill reverses its cost and its payments together.

6 · The branch, in full

Read top to bottom; the first match wins. The three unconditional skips above it — payroll‑linked, already posted, count‑linked — are unchanged and are not repeated here.

RowPostsWhy

The replay carries the identical predicate, in two places, and a check pins them.

unposted_ledger_source_rows (what the Post History door counts) and backfill_shop_ledger step 1 (what the replay writes) both grow the same exists clause against invoices.stock_receipt_id. If they disagree the door promises entries the replay will not write, which is the failure verify-backfill.sql exists to catch in both directions. unposted_inventory_movement and opening_inventory_gap read the view rather than the base tables, so they inherit it and need no edit — which is what that layer was split out for.

No existing row carries a link on the day this ships, so the replay writes exactly what it wrote yesterday for every row that exists. The new arm can only affect rows created after it.

7 · What is still open, stated rather than hidden

A linked bill whose amount differs from the delivery's value.

The delivery credited 2000 by its costed value; paying the bill debits 2000 by the bill's. A carriage charge or a rounding leaves the difference standing in Accounts Payable for ever. The form prefills the amount and warns when it is edited, which is the whole defence — posting the difference honestly needs a goods‑received‑not‑invoiced or price‑variance account, and inventing one is phase 3. It is a small residue where the old one was unbounded.

One bill, several deliveries — or the other way round.

The column is one receipt per bill, and unique, so a bill covering three deliveries can name one. The other two stay unbilled and their payables stand until something clears them. A join table is the real answer and is not this task; the unique index at least stops two bills quietly settling the same delivery, which would drive 2000 negative by one delivery's whole value.

A bill that arrives before the goods.

Refused, with the reason. There is no delivery to name yet, and a payable for inventory the shop does not hold is a prepayment rather than a stock purchase. The remedy is to enter it when the goods land. Named here because a refusal a user cannot explain is how a feature stops being used.

A goods bill entered under another category and NOT linked.

Still doubles the payable. The door cannot require a link for supplies — a supplies bill usually is a supplies bill — so all that stands here is that the picker is offered on every category and the delivery's own reference number is printed in it. A mitigation, not a fix, and the only one available without asking the shop a question it should not have to answer.
