1 · The blue system — three roles, two blues

The treatment is right: a solid blue for the one real action, a tinted blue for secondary actions, a tinted chip for icons. The mistake is using one blue for all three. A colour that carries white at 4.5:1 is far too light to be text on its own wash.

The failure, precisely. In the proposal, tinted buttons set #0071E3 on #E8F2FE — that is 4.15:1, under the 4.5 floor for normal text. It is not an edge case: it is "View list", "Run report", "+Discount", "+ Discount the order" and the "Cash · $18.00" row — the most numerous controls on both screens, at 12–13px.

Accounting is not a redesign — it is a repoint

It is all there in grey. ledger-hub.tsx and reports-hub.tsx share one style block, so going blue is five values in two files, plus the tab pill.

One thing to decide rather than assume: reports-hub.tsx has no solid variant at all — every reports card is tinted, because none of them create anything. That is correct and should survive the recolour. If solid blue starts appearing on Reports cards, the "one fill per region" rule below has already been lost.

Two rules that keep it readable as it spreads

  • One fill per screen region. On the sale panel, Charge is the only solid blue — every other blue control is tinted. The moment a second solid button appears beside it, neither is "the" action. The Accounting cards obey this too: one solid per card, at most.
  • Status colour is not the accent. Profit green, loss red and the refund amber keep their own tokens and their mandatory glyph. A screen where blue means "press" and green means "good" stays legible; one where blue means both does not.

3 · Where blue stops: the storefront

The admin is kaiibi's. The storefront is the shop's, and it renders in one of seven merchant-chosen palettes for a stranger with no account.

Kaiibi blue crosses onto the shop page in exactly one place: the checkout affordance (decided 2026-09-05). The slip's Checkout button and the place-order button are #0071E3 on every palette — a fixed transactional colour, the same pattern WhatsApp green already sets: committing an order goes through kaiibi whichever shop you are in, so that one moment wears one recognisable colour. Everything else on the shop page stays the shop's: Add buttons, the active category pill, tints, type — all palette-drawn. A shop that wants the whole page blue picks Azure (§4), where page and affordance agree byte-for-byte.

Worth recording, since it came up: the storefront already matches the Accounting tab's surface system. Page tone, card fill, radii and the borderless treatment are identical values, not merely similar — theme-shared.tsx says so in its own header. Only the colours differ, and that difference is the feature.

Two desktop facts folded into the plan

The column widens. The whole shop is capped at SHOP_MAX_WIDTH 1080 — on a 1,600px+ window that is a strip with dead gutters. Plan Task 8 raises it to 1320, the admin shell's own desktop ceiling, and gives the product grid a fifth column past 1280px so tiles stay near the ~250px they were designed at. The slip, the tab rail and all three theme scrollers read the same constant, so nothing else moves by hand.

Long catalogues already scroll safely. Market and Window render a virtualized FlatList (only visible tiles mount, however many products exist); Counter is a plain price-list scroll built for length. Finding tools kick in as the list grows: search appears at 12+ items, and the category band jumps the list. The one unbounded piece is the fetch — the public read returns the whole catalogue in one call — fine at hundreds of products, noted in the plan as its own future task at thousands.

6 · Four more improvements

1 · Palm paints "Buy" and "Message us" the same green

Defect
Observed
Palm's accent #1F6B45 against the fixed WhatsApp button #1F7A4D — ΔE 7.51, half the codebase's own ΔE 15 floor. Every other palette sits 49–88 away.
Fix
Two values in one row. Accent → #14543A (ΔE 19.07, white-text contrast improves 6.47:1 → 8.90:1), and ink → #15191B — charcoal instead of green-black, so the dark surfaces stop being a green card under a green button (see §7's four-way gallery).

2 · The shop name renders over a grey shape, and the tagline renders twice

Defect
Observed
On the live shop: a grey angular block behind the headline, and the tagline printed twice — bold over the shape, then again as plain text.
Why it matters
It is the anchor card, the first thing a stranger reads, and a duplicated line reads as broken software on a page whose whole job is looking trustworthy enough to walk to.
Status
Not yet traced.

3 · Open/closed state disappears at the door

Worth doing
Observed
isOpenAt is called in exactly two places — the Visit panel and shop-directory-card.tsx. A customer sees Open now in the directory and loses it the moment they enter the shop.
Fix
Surface it in the slip — "collection · opens 8am". Zero new data.

4 · The accent is spent on decoration as well as action

Polish
Observed
On the shop page the accent fills the checkout bar, the active category pill, the Add buttons, the Open now pill and the nine stock dots.
Why
The dots are a readout, not something you press — the same "one fill per region" rule as §2.
Fix
Draw in-stock dots in ink, matching what the catalogue already does for in-stock text. Out-of-stock keeps its ring.
Deliberately not listed: the empty middle of the "In the shop" card. It looks like a rhythm problem and is not — marginTop: 'auto' is doing it on purpose, with a comment explaining that the alternative is dead air at the bottom.

Removed on Pro — three constraints from the code

  • It cannot use the entitlements path. The storefront renders with no session; my_shop_entitlements() is authed. The flag travels on get_public_storefront, the security definer RPC that already decides what an anonymous visitor sees.
  • Do not test planKey === 'pro'. Plans get retired and hopped to a successor, and post_trial_plan_key can move. Model it as a capability on the plan row, as plans.modules already is.
  • Default to showing branding. entitlements.ts's own rule — over-permitting "is the shape a monetization bypass takes". The perk is hiding the mark, so Boolean(row.hide_branding) turning a missing column into false is correct by construction.

A shop in grace is fully usable by design, so branding should stay hidden through grace and return on expired — another reason to compute it server-side rather than in a client that has no business knowing about billing.
