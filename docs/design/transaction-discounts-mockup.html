Showing what was taken off a sale

Today an opened sale lists "2× QA scanwidget … $17.98" and nothing else. You can't tell a discount happened, how much it was, or what it was off. This adds the list price, the amount and % off, and the reason on every item. It also adds a summary for the whole sale.

All three examples are real sales from yusefshop, and their figures add up to the stored totals.

dashed outline = new on this screen

1 · The list says a sale was discounted before you open it

A blue tag beside the refund badge. It shows the total taken off and what share of the list price that was. Sales with no discount get no tag.

Decisions for you

% of what?

Recommended: % of the list price. An item's % is its discount over list price × quantity. The tag and the "customer saved" line use all discounts over the whole list price. The one exception is the sale discount line, which uses what was left after item discounts, because that's the amount it was taken off.

The tag in the list

Recommended: amount and % ("−$3.90 · 10%"). The alternative is % only, which is shorter but hides that a 50% discount on a $1 item hardly matters.

Loyalty points

Points redeemed are money off too. Recommended: show them on their own summary line, but leave them out of the tag's %, since the customer paid for them with earlier purchases rather than getting a price cut.

Receipts

Not in this mockup. The printed and WhatsApp receipt could use the same item and summary lines; say if you want it drawn too.
