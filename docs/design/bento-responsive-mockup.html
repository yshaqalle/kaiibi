Sizing by card, not by device

The grid currently picks its column count from fixed pixel breakpoints, so every new screen size is a case someone has to find by hand. The proposal replaces that with one rule: a bento card is never narrower than 240pt. Drag the width to compare — all seven Dashboard zones, not just the strip at the top.

Why the current rule keeps needing another patch

The ladder is 12 → 6 → 1 with no middle step, and dropping from twelve columns to six does not widen a small card at all: span={2} resolves to one sixth of the grid at both counts. That is why the revenue spark card kept breaking “Revenue” across two lines no matter which breakpoint moved.

What the proposed rule does

A cell keeps the proportion the design asked for whenever that clears the floor. When it does not, it widens to the next fraction that does — a third, a half, then full width — and the row wraps on its own. Wide screens are untouched; only cramped ones degrade, and they degrade the same way everywhere.

The tuning surface becomes a single number you can argue about on design grounds, rather than a list of devices someone has to keep discovering. Sizes neither of us has opened are correct by construction.
